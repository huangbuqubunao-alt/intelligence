## Schema 35 necessity

A completed timed Content has ORIGINAL_TRANSCRIPT coverage and additional
Summary-promoted EXTRACTION spans. Distillation seals only canonical coverage,
but schema 34's no-new-result trigger counts every transcript-backed Evidence
row. A valid NO_NEW_INTELLIGENCE response therefore fails durable admission
after extractive organization. Migration 35 replaces this one trigger to require
the exact ORIGINAL_TRANSCRIPT set. It preserves all Evidence, Sources, Videos,
historical results and schema-34 checksums. No table/data rewrite or provider
authorization change is required. New/upgrade/restart/rollback coverage and
owner-copy startup validation precede owner supported migration.
